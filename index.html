01 / About

Mathematics with a focus on rigorous PDE analysis.

I am an undergraduate student in Mathematics at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), with an expected B.S. degree in 2027.

My interests lie mainly in analysis and partial differential equations, especially mathematical questions arising from incompressible fluid dynamics. I am particularly interested in the Navier–Stokes equations, Liouville-type problems, and regularity theory.

This website collects public research directions, mathematical notes, and selected academic experience. Unpublished arguments and collaborative drafts are kept private until they are ready to be shared.

02 / Research

Research interests

Problems where qualitative structure, scaling, and regularity interact.

A

Navier–Stokes equations

Qualitative properties of incompressible viscous flows, with emphasis on rigorous analytical questions.

B

Liouville-type theorems

Rigidity, classification, and nonexistence questions under decay, integrability, or scale-sensitive assumptions.

C

Regularity theory

Critical spaces, blow-up ideas, a priori estimates, and mechanisms that connect local information to global structure.

Current direction

Liouville-type questions for the three-dimensional stationary Navier–Stokes equations and related regularity problems.

Jun. 2026 — Present Research project

Conditional Liouville theorems for the Navier–Stokes equations

Research on rigidity questions for finite-energy solutions of the three-dimensional stationary Navier–Stokes equations.

Advisor: Prof. Jincheng Yang, Johns Hopkins University
