01 / About

Mathematics with a focus on rigorous PDE analysis.

I am an undergraduate student at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). My current interests lie mainly in analysis and partial differential equations, especially questions arising from incompressible fluid mechanics.

I use this website to collect public research directions, mathematical notes, and links to ongoing academic work. Unpublished arguments and collaborative drafts are kept private until they are ready to be shared.

02 / Research

Research interests

Problems where qualitative structure, scaling, and regularity interact.

A

Navier–Stokes equations

Qualitative properties of incompressible viscous flows, with emphasis on analytical questions rather than numerical simulation.

B

Liouville-type theorems

Rigidity, classification, and nonexistence questions under decay, integrability, or scale-sensitive assumptions.

C

Regularity theory

Critical spaces, blow-up ideas, a priori estimates, and mechanisms that connect local information to global structure.

Current direction

Liouville-type questions for the Navier–Stokes equations and related regularity problems.
