01 / About

Mathematics with a focus on rigorous PDE analysis.

I am an undergraduate student in Mathematics at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), with an expected B.S. degree in 2027.

My interests lie mainly in analysis and partial differential equations, especially mathematical questions arising from incompressible fluid dynamics. I am particularly interested in the Navier–Stokes equations, Liouville-type problems, and regularity theory.

This website collects public research directions, mathematical notes, and selected academic experience. Unpublished arguments and collaborative drafts are kept private until they are ready to be shared.

02 / Research

Research interests

Analysis, partial differential equations, and fluid dynamics, with an emphasis on rigorous structure and regularity.

A

Analysis

Analytical structures, estimates, compactness, and qualitative ideas that form the foundation of my work in PDE.

B

Partial Differential Equations

Qualitative behavior, regularity, rigidity, and scale-sensitive phenomena in nonlinear partial differential equations.

C

Fluid Dynamics

Mathematical questions arising from incompressible flows, especially the Navier–Stokes equations and related fluid PDE.

Current direction

Liouville-type questions for the three-dimensional stationary Navier–Stokes equations and related regularity problems.
